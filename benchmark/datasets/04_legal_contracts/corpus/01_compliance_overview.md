# Compliance & Regulatory Overview: TechCorp Legal Framework

## Overview
TechCorp Holdings Inc. maintains strict compliance standards across all vendor agreements, service contracts, and subsidiary operations.

## Key Governance Rules
1. **Subcontractor Restrictions**: No vendor (including Logistics Plus LLC) may delegate contract duties without explicit written consent.
2. **Patent and IP Assignment**: All rail and logistics optimizations developed in partnership with BlueRail Freight Corp are subject to strict IP transfer clauses.
3. **Board Authority**: Only the TechCorp Holdings Board of Directors has the legal authority to grant exceptions to exclusivity clauses in Master Service Agreement MSA-2024-88.

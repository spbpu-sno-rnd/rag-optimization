# Hospital Department Directory and Specialist Roster

City General Hospital operates specialized outpatient and inpatient divisions to manage complex multi-system disorders.

## Department of Cardiology
- **Head of Department**: Dr. Elena Rostova, MD.
- **Specialty**: Interventional cardiology, resistant hypertension, and heart failure management.
- **Associated Facilities**: Cardiac Intensive Care Unit (CICU), Outpatient Diagnostic Center.

## Department of Nephrology
- **Head of Department**: Dr. Marcus Thorne, MD.
- **Specialty**: Glomerular diseases, chronic kidney disease (CKD) staging, and renal replacement therapies.
- **Collaborations**: Dr. Marcus Thorne frequently consults with Dr. Elena Rostova on cardiorenal syndrome cases originating from the Cardiology department.

## Endocrinology Clinic
- **Lead Specialist**: Dr. Sarah Jenkins, MD.
- **Specialty**: Diabetes mellitus, metabolic syndrome, and endocrine hypertension screening.

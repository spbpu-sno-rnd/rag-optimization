# Clinical Trial & Research Notes: Cardiorenal Outcomes 2024

## Overview
Recent clinical trials conducted at City General Hospital evaluated the progression of Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD Stage III) in hypertensive patients over 58 years of age.

## Key Findings
- Patients maintained on unmonitored high-dose ACE inhibitors while showing eGFR levels below 50 experienced a 25% faster decline in renal filtration capacity.
- Multidisciplinary oversight between Dr. Elena Rostova (Cardiology) and Dr. Marcus Thorne (Nephrology) successfully mitigated adverse drug reactions by adjusting baseline therapies.

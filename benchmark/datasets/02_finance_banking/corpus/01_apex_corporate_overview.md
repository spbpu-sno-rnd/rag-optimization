# Apex Holdings Corporate Structure and Regulatory Filings

Apex Holdings Corp is a publicly traded financial holding company controlling three major operating entities:
1. **FinTech Solutions Sub**: Core payment gateway, recurring SaaS, and subscription billing services.
2. **Apex Capital Markets**: Institutional trading, market making, and prime brokerage.
3. **Vanguard Insurance Sub**: Underwriter of commercial liability and reinsurance policies.

## Intercompany Ownership and Debt Guarantees
Apex Holdings Corp guarantees up to $50.0 Million of the Meridian Commercial Bank credit line for Apex Capital Markets.
However, Apex Holdings has explicitly **not** guaranteed the $150.0 Million Global Credit Corp facility held directly by FinTech Solutions Sub.

## Key Audit Findings (2024)
- **Primary Auditor**: Ernst & Young LLP.
- **Audit Opinion**: Unqualified opinion with explanatory paragraph regarding FinTech Solutions' upcoming debt maturity in April 2025 ($45.0M Bridge Loan).
- **Consolidated Net Profit**: Consolidated FY 2024 Net Income across all entities reached $36.6 Million after tax expenses.

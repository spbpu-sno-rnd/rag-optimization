# Legal Audit Report: Oakwood Settlement and Compliance Analysis

## Executive Summary
This document summarizes the pending legal disputes involving Apex Holdings Corp and its operating subsidiaries as of December 2024.

## Case Details: Oakwood Retail Group vs. FinTech Solutions
- **Jurisdiction**: US District Court for the Southern District of New York.
- **Claim**: Oakwood Retail Group claims FinTech Solutions incorrectly applied tier-3 interchange rates to standard transaction processing, overcharging merchants over a 14-month period between 2022 and 2023.
- **Legal Defense**: FinTech Solutions maintains that fee structures were explicitly allowed under Section 4.2 of the Master Merchant Agreement.
- **Settlement Status**: In November 2024, mediation began with an escrow offer of $12.5 Million proposed by Apex Holdings' legal counsel to avert trial.

## Regulatory Regulatory Notice: CFPB Advisory Notice
The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) issued an inquiry into Vanguard Insurance Sub concerning payment protection insurance bundled with consumer loans.
